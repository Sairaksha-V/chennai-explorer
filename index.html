OUR BUSINESS

Chennai Explorer Business Model

Chennai Explorer is designed as a digital tourism marketplace connecting visitors with destinations, experiences and local tourism businesses.

👥

Target Customers

Tourists, students, families, local residents, working professionals and visitors looking to explore Chennai.

🏢

Business Partners

Hotels, restaurants, activity providers, tour operators, local businesses and experience providers.

🌐

Digital Platform

A website that brings destination information, local experiences and future booking opportunities together.

🚀

Future Expansion

The platform can later introduce hotel bookings, activity reservations, travel services and online payments.

OUR VISION

Making Chennai easier to discover.

To become a convenient digital platform for discovering Chennai's destinations, experiences and local attractions.

OUR MISSION

Connecting people with Chennai.

To provide organized and accessible destination information while creating opportunities for local tourism businesses and service providers.

BUSINESS STRATEGY

Revenue Model

Chennai Explorer can generate revenue through multiple digital tourism and partnership-based channels.

01

Featured Listings

Tourism businesses can pay to receive enhanced visibility and featured placement on the platform.

02

Booking Commission

The platform can earn a commission from eligible hotel, activity and experience bookings generated through partner businesses.

03

Affiliate Revenue

Revenue can be earned by referring users to selected travel and tourism services through affiliate partnerships.

04

Sponsored Places

Local businesses can sponsor promotional placements to reach relevant visitors.

05

Digital Advertising

Relevant tourism businesses can advertise products and services to users of the platform.

06

Premium Business Plans

Businesses can subscribe to premium profiles offering additional visibility and promotional features.

Current Status: Chennai Explorer is currently a website prototype. The revenue streams shown above represent the planned future business model and are not currently processing real payments.

TRUST & SAFETY

Privacy & Security

Protecting customer information and maintaining trust are important parts of Chennai Explorer's future e-commerce operations.

🔐

Privacy Protection

  • Minimal Data Collection: Collect only information necessary for the service.
  • Purpose Transparency: Explain why customer information is collected and used.
  • Data Protection: Protect personal information from unauthorized access.
  • User Rights: Provide appropriate mechanisms for users to manage their personal information.
  • No Unnecessary Sharing: Personal information will not be sold or shared without an appropriate legal or business basis.
🛡️

Website Security

  • HTTPS: Secure encrypted communication for the website.
  • Secure Authentication: Future customer accounts will use secure authentication mechanisms.
  • Password Protection: Passwords will be securely hashed rather than stored in plain text.
  • Secure Payments: Future payments will use trusted payment gateways instead of storing card information directly.
  • Input Validation: User inputs will be validated to reduce common security risks.
  • Backups: Important business data will be backed up regularly.
🔒
Security by Design

Privacy and security will be considered throughout the development and operation of the platform.
