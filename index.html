Chennai Explorer Business Model

Chennai Explorer is designed as a digital tourism marketplace connecting visitors with destinations, experiences and local tourism businesses.

Target Customers

Tourists, students, families, local residents, business travellers and visitors looking for places and experiences in Chennai.

Value Proposition

One convenient digital platform where users can discover Chennai attractions, understand important information and connect with tourism-related services.

Key Partners

Local tourism businesses, restaurants, hotels, travel agencies, event organisers, guides and experience providers.

Key Activities

Destination listing, content management, tourism information, digital promotion, customer support and platform maintenance.

Customer Relationship

Simple navigation, useful information, responsive support, reviews and personalised recommendations.

Distribution Channels

Website, mobile-friendly platform, social media marketing, search engines and partnerships with local businesses.

Our Vision

To become a convenient digital gateway for discovering Chennai and promoting its tourism, culture, heritage and local experiences.

Our Mission

To provide users with accessible, organised and reliable information about Chennai while creating digital opportunities for local tourism businesses.

Revenue Model

Chennai Explorer can generate revenue through multiple digital tourism and e-commerce channels.

Featured Listings

Tourism businesses can pay to promote their attractions, services or experiences on the platform.

Commission

The platform can earn a commission from bookings made through hotels, restaurants, tours or experience providers.

Advertising

Relevant tourism businesses can advertise their products and services to platform users.

Partnership Plans

Businesses can purchase premium partnership packages for greater visibility and promotional opportunities.

Future Revenue Opportunity: The platform can introduce premium travel packages, guided experiences, event bookings and affiliate partnerships as the business grows.

Taxation & Financial Compliance

A commercial version of Chennai Explorer would need appropriate financial and tax compliance based on its business structure, revenue model and applicable Indian regulations.

GST

Applicable services and transactions would need to follow GST registration, invoicing and reporting requirements wherever applicable.

Income Tax

Business income would be accounted for and reported according to the applicable income-tax rules.

Financial Records

The platform would maintain appropriate records of revenue, expenses, invoices, commissions and other financial transactions.

Privacy & Security

User trust and protection of information are important for a digital tourism e-commerce platform.

Data Privacy

Collect only necessary user information and clearly explain how information is used.

Secure Payments

A commercial platform should use trusted payment gateways and secure payment processing methods.

Account Security

User accounts should use secure passwords, authentication controls and appropriate access management.

Website Security

HTTPS, secure coding practices, regular updates and protection against common web attacks should be implemented.

Data Protection

Personal and business information should be protected from unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure.

User Awareness

Users should be provided with clear privacy information, terms of use and safe online transaction guidance.

About Chennai Explorer

Chennai Explorer is an academic e-commerce business concept focused on digital tourism. The platform combines destination discovery with opportunities for local tourism businesses to reach potential customers.

The project demonstrates how a simple web-based platform can combine business planning, technology, digital marketing, revenue generation, privacy and security considerations.

Future versions could include online bookings, user accounts, reviews, personalised recommendations, travel packages and mobile applications.
